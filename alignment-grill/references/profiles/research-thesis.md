# Research or thesis

One student or a small group with a supervisor; the claims matter more than the code.

- **Lease:** 3d
- **Decision deadline:** 1 week, or the next supervisor meeting
- **Extra task fields:** Chapter, Claim (TEXT: the claim it supports)
- **Default grouping:** Chapter
- **Default stages:** plan, implement, review for experiments; implement for writing
- **Who votes:** the people involved; research questions and claims, the supervisor too when they are on GitHub
- **Extra kickoff questions:**
  - What are the research questions, and what result would answer each?
  - Which baselines must every experiment beat?
  - Which data is fixed for the final evaluation, and who may look at it?
  - What has the supervisor already decided?

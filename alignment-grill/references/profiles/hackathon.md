# Hackathon

A team of 2 to 5 with agents, 24 to 48 hours, judged at the end.

- **Lease:** 2h
- **Decision deadline:** 3 hours after posting
- **Extra task fields:** Demo-critical (yes/no)
- **Default grouping:** Owner
- **Default stages:** implement; implement, review for anything demo-critical
- **Who votes:** the people involved; the goal and the demo script, everyone
- **Extra kickoff questions:**
  - What does the judging rubric reward, item by item, and which tasks serve each item?
  - What is the demo script, minute by minute?
  - What is the freeze time after which only fixes go in?
  - Which claim will the judges hear, and what number backs it?

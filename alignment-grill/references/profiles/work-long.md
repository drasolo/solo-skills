# Work or long project

A team over weeks or months, with reviews and releases.

- **Lease:** 1d
- **Decision deadline:** 3 working days after posting
- **Extra task fields:** Milestone, Priority (SINGLE_SELECT: P0, P1, P2)
- **Default grouping:** Milestone
- **Default stages:** plan, implement, review for features; implement, review for fixes
- **Who votes:** the people involved; architecture and the goal, everyone
- **Extra kickoff questions:**
  - Who outside the team can change the goal, and how do their requests arrive?
  - What must never break (data, users, integrations)?
  - What is the release process, and who approves a release?
  - Which documents already hold decisions, and do they move into decision issues?

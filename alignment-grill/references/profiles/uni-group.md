# University group project

Students with a fixed deadline, a grading scheme, and rules on AI use.

- **Lease:** 3d
- **Decision deadline:** 2 days after posting
- **Extra task fields:** Deadline (DATE), Grade weight (TEXT)
- **Default grouping:** Deliverable
- **Default stages:** implement, review
- **Who votes:** the people involved; the topic, scope and deliverables, everyone
- **Extra kickoff questions:**
  - What does the course allow AI to do, and what must each student write or explain alone?
  - What does each deliverable get graded on, and how much is it worth?
  - Who presents which part, and must each person be able to explain the whole?
  - Which deadlines are fixed, and which are set by the team?

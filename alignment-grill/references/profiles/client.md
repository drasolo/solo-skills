# Client work

A team delivering to a client; the scope is a promise.

- **Lease:** 1d
- **Decision deadline:** 2 working days, or the next client call
- **Extra task fields:** Deliverable, Billable (yes/no), In scope (yes/no)
- **Default grouping:** Deliverable
- **Default stages:** plan, implement, review
- **Who votes:** the people involved; scope changes, everyone, recorded as verbal decisions when the client agreed in a call
- **Extra kickoff questions:**
  - What exactly did the client sign for, and what is explicitly out?
  - Who on the client side can approve a change?
  - Which client data or systems can agents touch, and which never?
  - When are the checkpoints with the client?
